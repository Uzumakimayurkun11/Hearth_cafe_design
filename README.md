# Hearth Café — UI/UX Design

**Hearth Café** is a warm, welcoming mobile app for discovering coffee, tea, and fresh bakes. Browse the menu, customize a drink, and follow a simple flow from cart to checkout and pickup. A soft cream palette, espresso tones, and caramel accents give the experience the cozy feel of a neighborhood café.
